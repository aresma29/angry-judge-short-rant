A short, furious rant about the office microwave that refuses to stop beeping — one tiny everyday annoyance blown up into the most reliable relationship in the whole building.

> The office microwave beeps five times when the food is done, then beeps again every minute forever, like it is personally offended I have not thanked it yet — I reheated my lunch three hours ago and it is still out there, beeping, guarding a plate of cold noodles no one will ever eat, and somehow this is the most reliable relationship in the building.

`main.py` prints the line from `entry.txt`.
